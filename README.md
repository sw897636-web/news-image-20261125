A single 1024×1024 infographic summing up the world's news of **Wednesday 25 November 2026** — an entry for the [make.fun bounty](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261125).

## The six stories

1. **MIDDLE EAST · RIYADH — DRONE STRIKE HITS RIYADH'S TALLEST TOWER — AGAIN** — A Houthi drone hit the 80-storey, 385 m PIF Tower, the second attack in a week. Eleven people hurt, two seriously; the Saudi-led coalition calls it a terrorist attack.
2. **CHINA · POLITICS — SHANGHAI VOWS LOYALTY AFTER TOP OFFICIAL PROBED** — Shanghai party chief Chen Jining is under investigation for serious discipline violations, Xinhua said. City leaders pledged loyalty to Beijing.
3. **USA · TECH — HUAWEI FOUND GUILTY ON 10 OF 12 COUNTS** — A Brooklyn jury convicted Huawei of bank and wire fraud, sanctions violations and trade-secret theft. The racketeering charge failed; sentencing is set for 14 September 2027.
4. **LATIN AMERICA — HOSTAGE STANDOFF IN CARTAGENA** — A gunman fled a van robbery into a Cartagena Exito supermarket, holding about 25 people after freeing five older women.
5. **GLOBAL · SOCIETY — ONE WOMAN KILLED EVERY HOUR IN 2025** — A UN report finds that on average five women an hour were killed by a partner or a relative in 2025.
6. **USA · LIGHTER SIDE — TRUMP PARDONS THANKSGIVING TURKEY** — The White House held its annual Thanksgiving turkey pardon. Thanksgiving falls on 26 November.

## How it works

`main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only) copies `entry.png` to the output directory (`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR`, default `/output`). The copy is byte-for-byte, so two runs produce identical output. `render.py` (Pillow, DejaVu fonts) draws the infographic from the verified facts listed above.

## Files

- `main.ts` — the entry program
- `entry.png` — the 1024×1024 infographic (124,909 bytes)
- `render.py` — the script that draws `entry.png`
- `preview-300.png` — the same image scaled to 300×300 for a small-size legibility check
- `LICENSE` — MIT
